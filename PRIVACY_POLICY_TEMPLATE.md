# Reverse Snake Privacy Policy

Publisher: **Samir Dubey**  
Contact: **contact2sdcode@gmail.com**  
Effective date: **26/09/2026**

## The game and local data

Reverse Snake is a free arcade game. You do not need an app account. Scores, settings, selected cosmetics and the choice to enable online features are stored on your device. The game also stores signed Google Play purchase receipts so previously verified purchases can remain available offline. We do not operate a gameplay analytics or account server for this version.

Clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove this local data. This does not cancel or erase purchase records held by Google Play. Purchases may be restored through the same Google Play account.

## Optional online features

Online features are enabled by default on new installations and can be disabled in Settings. Existing offline choices are retained on upgrade. We do not collect or infer a birth date or age through this setting. Ad requests are subject to the privacy choices and consent requirements handled by Google UMP. The local connectivity preference is separate from ad consent. Disabling them stops new requests made by the game; it does not recall data already processed by Google.

Google AdMob supplies advertisements. Its SDK may process IP-derived general location, ad/app interactions, diagnostic information and device identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It uses encryption in transit. Google UMP manages applicable consent messages and privacy choices. Where required, use Settings → Ad privacy choices to review your choices. Your Android advertising-ID controls also apply.

Google Play handles payments, order records and purchase restoration. The game receives product and purchase-ownership information, not your payment-card details. Prices and payment confirmation are shown by Google Play. Optional rewarded ads provide one in-game revive after the ad provider confirms the reward.

Google processes data under its own terms and privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . Information about Google's advertising controls is available at https://safety.google/privacy/ads-and-data/ . Third-party retention is governed by those services, rather than by the game's local storage settings.

## Permissions and support

Internet/network access supports consent, ads and purchases. The advertising SDK may use the advertising-ID permission. The game does not request access to precise location, contacts, microphone, camera or shared photos.

If you email support, the publisher receives the information you send and uses it to answer your request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers or private signing credentials. Contact the publisher above for questions about this policy or the handling of your support correspondence.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its services change. The effective date will identify the current policy. Material changes to data use will be reflected in the app and its store disclosures as applicable.
